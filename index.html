      In Summary

      Two public indicators — depression prevalence and a composite economic-stress measure — explain roughly 72% of state-level variation in mental distress, predicting within ±0.53 percentage points on held-out states. A reproducible baseline built entirely on data CDC already publishes.

      CDCpublic data → 2predictors → 51state estimates → 0.53RMSE (pp)
      5

      Read it carefully.

      1. Small sample n = 52 (50 states + DC + national aggregate). Confidence intervals on single-state predictions will be wide.
      2. Cross-sectional Relationships are correlational, not causal.
      3. Ecological State averages don't apply to individuals. Within-state variation is enormous.
      4. Single hold-out No cross-validation; the generalization estimate is noisy.
